Department of Computer Application

The Department of Computer Applications (MCA) at Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET), located in Mulavoor, Muvattupuzha, Kerala, was established in 2008. The department is focused on developing software professionals capable of adapting to the global IT sector

Curriculum & Facilities

The program blends theoretical knowledge with mandatory core laboratory sessions and real-world project work. The final semester is completely dedicated to live project work and specialization selection. The department frequently organizes technical seminars, workshops, and industrial visits to bridge the gap between academic theory and actual industry standards
